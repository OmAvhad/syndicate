## 🎯 Market Opportunity: Social Copy Trading
* **Global Copy Trading Volume:** Over \$100B+ annually across centralized platforms like eToro, Binance, and Bybit.
* **The Web3 Problem:** Onchain copy-trading has struggled to scale because public mempools and block explorers expose leaders' trades, allowing free scrapers and sandwich bots to destroy alpha.

## 🚀 How SyndicateCopy Drives Arkiv Protocol Adoption

1. **High Transaction Throughput via Ephemeral Signals:**
   * Every active trader generates 5–20 trade signals per week.
   * With 10,000 active copy traders, this represents **50,000 to 200,000 ephemeral entities per week** written to Arkiv.
   * Because signals automatically expire after 10 minutes, Arkiv provides high throughput without exponential state bloat.

2. **Decentralized Subscription Epochs:**
   * Millions of follower relationships expiring and renewing on a rolling 30-day epoch establish Arkiv as the canonical standard for decentralized memberships and access control.

3. **Verifiable Audit Records for All Trading Protocols:**
   * Any social trading platform, DAO treasury, or hedge fund can query Arkiv's `trade_promises` schema to audit any leader's historical performance with zero possibility of retroactive tampering.
